## Proposed policy text

Mini Shop Tycoon works offline. It does not require an account and does not send
gameplay data to a server. It contains no advertising or analytics service.

Progress and sound preferences are stored on your device. The game does not
collect or share personal information. Reset starts a new game while retaining
local recovery archives; uninstalling normally removes the app's local data.

Contact: to be completed by the developer before publication.

## Data safety preparation

The reviewed game has no network permission, login, analytics, advertising,
payments or third-party service integrations. Based on this code/artifact review,
the proposed disclosure is no data collected or shared. The owner must complete
