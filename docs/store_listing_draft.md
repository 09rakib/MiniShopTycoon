Title: Mini Shop Tycoon

Short description: Cook, carry and serve in your own little offline food shop.

Description:

Build a little burger shop one delivery at a time. Touch and drag to move your
chef, collect freshly made food automatically and serve hungry customers. Walk
to the cash point to collect your earnings, improve your burger machine and hire
a stocker to help move burgers.

Unlock a fries machine and add a table and chairs. Keep track of your stock,
carry up to two items and grow at your own pace. The earlier grocery business is
also available from the menu.

Play offline, with no account or ads. Progress and your sound preference stay on
your device. Furniture currently provides seating decoration; customer dining
is not yet part of gameplay.
